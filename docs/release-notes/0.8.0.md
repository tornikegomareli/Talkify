- Added Prompt Shaping, a beta. Turn on **Shape dictation with a prompt** in **Settings → Prompt Shaping** and the prompt you pick rewrites finished dictation through Apple's on-device model before it is inserted. Three prompts are built in: tighten grammar, bullet lists, and remove filler words.
- The prompt library shows each prompt's instruction under its name, so you pick by reading rather than by remembering what three names meant. Edit any of them or write your own.
- A prompt can be tried before you use it. Type a sample sentence in the editor, press **Try**, and read what came back. It runs the same on-device model a real session runs, so writing a prompt is no longer guesswork.
- While you dictate, a caption under the notch names the prompt the session will shape with, colored from your Edge Glow palette and moving with your voice. The bare left and right arrow keys cycle through your prompts, or **None** to insert the words exactly as spoken, and that choice lasts for the session without changing what Settings says.
- Shaping fails safe. Any failure, refusal, or an answer slower than ten seconds inserts your raw words unchanged, transcription history keeps what you actually said, and nothing leaves your Mac. The framing that keeps the model rewriting your words instead of answering them is fixed and not editable.
- Shaping runs before translation, so a translated session shapes the words you spoke and then translates the result. History records the text that actually landed.
