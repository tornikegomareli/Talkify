- Added Intelligence, a new voice visual. Pick it in **Settings → Appearance → Voice visual**. When you start dictating, a drop of color forms under the camera, falls out of the notch, pinches off and settles as an orb beside your words; when you stop, it flows back up into the notch. A rim of color turns inside the shape and the live draft is laid over the same colors, all following your Edge Glow palette.
- The orb and rim breathe with your voice. If the microphone stops sending audio, they freeze in amber, the same signal the other visuals give, so a dead microphone never looks like silence.
- Intelligence keeps Edge Glow + Draft's live line of your last eight words, and like every visual it scales with **HUD size** and steps aside for a quiet level meter under Reduce Motion.
