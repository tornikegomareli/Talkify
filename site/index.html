How it works

Three stages,
all on device.

01

Capture

A global CGEvent tap watches for the trigger key and swallows it while bound, so the key never reaches the focused app. Press starts the session; release ends it; a release under 250 ms latches it hands-free. Audio comes from AVAudioEngine on the current system input.

02

Recognize

Apple's SpeechAnalyzer and SpeechTranscriber run the language model on the Neural Engine. The analyzer is prewarmed at launch, so the first frame of audio is already being processed. Volatile results render immediately and are replaced as recognition firms up.

03

Insert

The focused control is captured before recording via the Accessibility API, then written directly through AXUIElement on release. Apps that refuse it fall back to a clipboard paste that restores your previous clipboard, and the working route is remembered per app.

Privacy

Your transcriptions
stay on your Mac.

No account. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to sign into. Download it and dictate.
No internet. Recognition runs on the Neural Engine with Apple's on-device models. Talkify makes zero network requests, so airplane mode changes nothing.
Stored on your disk only. Your text is written to the app you were typing in and nowhere else. Insights are computed and kept locally, as a plain file on your own disk.
And then some

It reads back,
and it's yours to tune.

Read Aloud

Select text in any app and Talkify speaks it — with Apple's Enhanced and Premium voices, or with your own Personal Voice once you authorize it. Voices are listed with their language and quality, the list refreshes the moment a new one finishes downloading, and playback runs on device and offline.
