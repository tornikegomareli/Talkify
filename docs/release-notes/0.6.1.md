- Added mouse buttons as a dictation trigger. Bind Middle Click, or another auxiliary button, in Settings > Shortcuts. Left and right click are never bindable.
- A bound mouse button keeps its usual action unless the exact combination you bound is pressed, so binding option and Middle Click leaves plain middle click alone.
- Added a Launch at Login setting.
- Fixed the notch HUD not appearing, or animating wrongly, after the Mac had been idle for a few hours.
- Fixed two dictation triggers on the same key with different modifiers doing nothing, and a release ending the wrong one.
- Settings now says how a hands-free session ends: tap to start, tap again to finish.
