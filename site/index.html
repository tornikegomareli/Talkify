How it works

Hold. Speak.
Release.

That's the whole manual. No window to open, no mode to enter. Dictation is a gesture, not an app you switch to.

🌐fn
01

Hold fn

The HUD descends from the notch instantly. A quick tap latches the session for hands-free dictation. Any key can be the trigger — rebind it in seconds.

02

Say it

On-device Apple Speech transcribes as you talk, and the HUD's voice visual moves with you. Silence and a dead microphone never look the same.

Your words land in the control you were just using — any app, any text field. Accessibility insertion first, clipboard paste as the fallback, your clipboard restored.

Privacy

Your words never
leave your Mac.

No cloud. Recognition runs on the Neural Engine with Apple's on-device speech models. Airplane mode changes nothing.
No accounts. No telemetry. Talkify makes zero network requests. There is nothing to sign into and nothing phoning home.
No history. Audio and recognized text are never stored. The only record is the local usage numbers you can read yourself.
