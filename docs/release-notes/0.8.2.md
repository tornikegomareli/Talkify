- Fixed dictation hearing nothing when you use Bluetooth earbuds. Opening the microphone makes a headset switch to its hands-free profile, which stops the audio engine, and nothing restarted it: the notch came up, turned amber, and no words arrived. The engine is rebuilt when the audio route changes, so the session keeps listening through the switch.
- The notch no longer reports a dead microphone while the microphone is still opening. A headset takes about a second and a half to deliver its first audio, and that was being judged by the same short timeout that catches a microphone failing mid-sentence. Amber still means what it always did once audio has been heard.
- Dictation starts about a quarter of a second sooner on Bluetooth.
